Accuracy (θ)
How far away a group of stars must be before it counts as a single point. It is calculated as box size ÷ distance. If that number is below θ, the group is merged. If not, the box is opened and its smaller boxes are checked.
Tree boxes
Space is split into 8 cubes, and each cube into 8 more, until every cube holds at most one star. Crowded areas get small cubes.
Group as one point
A far-away box is replaced by one point at the average position of its stars, weighted by mass (the center of mass).
Fewer calculations
Checking every pair needs N × (N − 1) = calculations. Barnes-Hut did this frame, which is fewer.
Units
u = distance, t = time, m = mass. These are the simulation's own units, not real light-years, seconds or kilograms. Speed is u/t and force is m·u/t².
